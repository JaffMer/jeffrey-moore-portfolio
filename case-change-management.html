Business Challenge

Helping managers translate strategy into employee action.

Organizational changes often succeed or fail at the manager level. Employees look to their direct leaders for context, clarity, support, and consistent expectations. This experience prepares managers to identify resistance early and respond in ways that preserve trust while moving implementation forward.

Design Approach

Practical decisions inside a realistic change journey.

The course uses recognizable workplace situations, character-led dialogue, feedback, and reflection to move beyond change-management terminology. Learners practice what to say, what to observe, when to coach, and how to reinforce adoption without treating discipline as the first response.

Learning Experience

What the initiative develops.

Recognizing Resistance

Learners distinguish normal uncertainty from behavioral resistance by observing performance patterns, listening to employee feedback, and documenting objective evidence.

Communicating Through Change

Managers practice building rapport, explaining expectations, inviting employee perspective, and connecting day-to-day actions to the larger organizational purpose.

Sustaining Adoption

The experience emphasizes follow-up, reinforcement, development planning, peer updates, recognition, and proportionate accountability after implementation begins.
