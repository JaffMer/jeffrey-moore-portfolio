The Evergreen Ecosystem

Learning across the employee journey.

A connected corporate learning environment that follows one employee at key moments in his career, from onboarding to first-time management and organizational change.

I created Evergreen Home Services as a fictional organization where I could design connected learning experiences around real workplace needs. Across the ecosystem, I use eLearning, scenario-based learning, technology simulations, performance support, leadership development, and change management to show how different L&D solutions can work together.

Evergreen Academy

Connected learning initiatives.

The design strategy

The goal was to make the Evergreen Project feel intentional and connected, and Marcus was the key. By returning to him at different points in his career, I was able to design for different challenges while keeping the learner, the organization, and the larger employee experience connected. The result combines formal learning with realistic practice, performance support, and tools learners can use when the course is over.
