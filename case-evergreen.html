Design Challenge

How do you introduce new technicians to a new organization without overwhelming them with information?

The challenge was to create an onboarding experience that introduced Evergreen's culture, expectations, safety responsibilities, and learning systems while preparing employees to independently navigate Evergreen Academy and apply what they learned in realistic situations.

AudienceNewly hired service technicians
StatusCompleted
RoleInstructional designer & eLearning developer
DeliverablesRise course, Storyline simulation, knowledge checks, performance support

Project Overview

Building a complete onboarding pathway.

This onboarding experience was designed to give new employees a structured introduction to Evergreen without relying on passive information delivery. Short instructional content, narrated moments, guided exploration, workplace scenarios, and Marcus's experience as a new technician work together to introduce the organization while keeping learners actively involved.

Design Focus

From orientation to application.

I believe onboarding should move beyond passive, glorified introduction sessions. In this experience, learners explore company values and expectations, navigate a simulated LMS, locate resources, respond to training messages, and demonstrate understanding through practical scenario-based tasks. The goal is for learners to actively practice what they will need to know and do, rather than simply being told about it.

Learning Experience

What the project includes.

Rise 360 Course Structure

Structured modules introduce the organization, support teams, Evergreen Academy, customer-service expectations, safety responsibilities, and opportunities to apply what learners have learned.

Storyline 360 LMS Simulation

Learners complete realistic LMS tasks, including enrolling in role-based training, locating archived course materials, responding to a message, and downloading a completion certificate.

Scenario-Based Assessment

Marcus provides continuity throughout the experience while giving learners realistic situations where they can interpret what is happening, make decisions, and apply their onboarding knowledge.

Design Reflection

My goal was to create an onboarding experience where each element had a clear purpose. Rise 360 provides structure and foundational knowledge, Storyline gives learners a safe place to practice using the LMS, and scenario-based assessment asks them to apply what they have learned. Performance support then gives learners resources they can return to when they need help beyond the course.
