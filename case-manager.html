Business Challenge

Supporting the shift from individual contributor to manager.

High-performing employees are often promoted because of their technical expertise, but leading people requires a different set of behaviors. This experience helps first-time managers redefine their role, establish healthy boundaries, adapt their leadership approach, and coach employees with greater clarity and confidence.

Design Approach

Learning through Marcus's promotion journey.

Marcus, introduced as a new technician in the onboarding experience, returns after earning a promotion. Learners advise him through realistic decisions, observe the consequences of different choices, and connect management concepts to situations they are likely to encounter during their first months in role.

Learning Experience

What the initiative develops.

Manager and Leader Responsibilities

Learners distinguish operational management from people leadership, then discover through scenario practice that strong managers intentionally combine both.

Dynamic Leadership

Six leadership approaches are applied to business situations before learners explore Dynamic Leadership: adapting behaviors to the people, context, and desired outcome.

Coaching and Development

Managers practice timely feedback, professional boundaries, the SBI framework, and development planning supported by downloadable on-the-job resources.

Manager Toolkit

Performance support beyond the course.

The learning experience is paired with practical resources managers can use before coaching conversations and during employee-development planning.
